# LIQUID GLASS SKILL

Create a reusable Liquid Glass design system.

The visual effect should resemble premium translucent
glass interfaces with depth and light interaction.

DO NOT apply glass to every element.

Use glass strategically.

---

## MATERIAL

A glass surface can combine:

- translucent background
- backdrop blur
- subtle border
- internal highlight
- gradient reflection
- soft shadow
- ambient glow
- noise texture

---

## LAYERS

Use multiple visual layers:

1. Background
2. Ambient light
3. Glass surface
4. Reflection
5. Content
6. Interaction highlight

---

## INTERACTION

On pointer movement:

- subtly move highlight
- change reflection angle
- slightly shift gradient
- increase border luminosity

Do not create excessive movement.

---

## COMPONENTS

Create reusable:

GlassCard
GlassPanel
GlassButton
GlassNavbar
GlassModal
GlassInput
GlassBadge

---

## RESPONSIVE

Reduce blur and expensive effects on mobile.

---

## ACCESSIBILITY

Maintain readable text contrast.

Never allow glass effects to make text unreadable.
